About FNP Quest

What it is

FNP Quest is a mobile-friendly web study tool for Family Nurse Practitioner learners. It combines structured lessons, quizzes with explanations, mixed practice, and optional progress synchronization.

FNP Quest is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by AANPCB, ANCC, ANA, or AANP.

Content & corrections

Clinical knowledge and examination outlines change. Verify time-sensitive recommendations against current guidelines, local policy, official prescribing information, and your program requirements.

General feedback and correction reports may be submitted through the public project tracker. Do not include names, email addresses, patient information, clinical records, or other sensitive information.

Privacy

Effective: August 28, 2026

Data stored on this device

Without an account, FNP Quest stores guest learning progress in your browser, including XP, streak, study dates, completed lesson quizzes, missed-question review data, theme preference, and course display preferences. Signed-in progress is stored in a separate local area for each account so switching accounts does not transfer learning data.

Optional cloud account

If you create an account, Supabase Auth processes your email address and authentication information. FNP Quest stores an account identifier, XP, streak, freeze count, study dates, and quiz-result records so supported progress can synchronize across devices. Guest progress is never added to a cloud account automatically; an eligible signed-in user must explicitly confirm a one-time import. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not stored in this app’s source code.

Anonymous usage statistics

FNP Quest sends limited aggregate events to Supabase to count site visits, lesson opens, lesson-quiz completions, mixed-practice completions, and advanced-practice completions. These events contain an event name, optional lesson number, app version, server time, and a random identifier that changes each local calendar day. They do not contain an account ID, email address, XP, streak, quiz score, selected answer, mistake text, or other free-form content. This separate statistics connection does not use a signed-in learning account session.

What we do not request

Do not enter patient names, medical-record information, protected health information, or other clinical data. FNP Quest is not designed to receive or store patient data.

Service providers

The site is delivered through GitHub Pages. Optional authentication, cloud synchronization, and anonymous aggregate usage statistics use Supabase. FNP Quest does not include advertising trackers and does not sell personal information.

Your choices

You may use the core learning features without an account and may turn anonymous usage statistics off or on for this browser at any time. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals also disable these events. Clearing local learning data affects only this browser. Signed-in users can submit a private account-deletion request from the Account page; the request is stored in their private authentication metadata for manual processing.

Terms & Disclaimer

Effective: August 25, 2026

Educational use only

FNP Quest is a supplementary educational study tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations for an individual patient, clinical decision support, continuing-education credit, or professional certification.

No exam guarantee or affiliation

Use of FNP Quest does not guarantee eligibility for or success on any certification examination. FNP Quest is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by AANPCB, ANCC, ANA, or AANP. Official examination materials and current test-content outlines control.

Verify current information

Healthcare guidance, drug labeling, examination outlines, and standards of care change. Learners remain responsible for verifying current guidance, local requirements, and official prescribing information and for using qualified faculty or clinical supervision.

Emergencies and patient care
